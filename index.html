ABOUT G3O

Grounded in science.
Driven by place.

G3O Spatial Solutions is an independent, research-driven geospatial consultancy focused on GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and geospatial research support.

Combining scientific rigor with practical geospatial methods, G3O helps researchers, academic communities, institutions, and organizations make sense of spatial information and translate it into useful outputs.

Engagements are delivered remotely by default, with on-site work considered on a case-by-case basis.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Does G3O accept student thesis and research projects?

Yes. G3O can provide technical and methodological support for research involving GIS, remote sensing, and spatial analysis. Researchers remain responsible for their study design, interpretation, conclusions, and academic submissions.

Does G3O provide complete thesis or research writing?

No. G3O does not provide ghostwriting or complete academic work on behalf of students or researchers. Services focus on legitimate technical assistance, consultation, data processing, analysis support, and training.

Can clients outside Mindanao avail of services?

Yes. G3O focuses on Mindanao while accepting remote engagements from elsewhere in the Philippines, subject to project requirements and feasibility.

How are project fees determined?

Fees are customized according to scope, complexity, data requirements, deliverables, timeline, and technical effort. A project-specific quotation is provided after initial assessment.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with project complexity, data availability, analysis requirements, review cycles, and agreed deliverables. A schedule is included in the project proposal or quotation.

How does G3O protect client data?

Non-public client data and project materials are treated as confidential unless otherwise agreed. File-transfer, retention, and deletion arrangements are established for each engagement.

Who owns final maps, datasets, and project outputs?

Ownership of agreed final deliverables is defined in the project agreement. G3O retains ownership of pre-existing tools, templates, and reusable workflows. Third-party data and software remain subject to their own licenses.
